Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact information such as your full name, email address, and phone number, collected when you create an account or complete your professional profile.
  • Business verification documents such as your government-issued ID, insurance certificate, bond proof, contractor licenses, and certifications. These are collected during contractor onboarding to verify your identity and eligibility to provide home services.
  • Company information such as your business name, address, and EIN number, collected to verify your business and enable payments for completed jobs.
  • Photos and media captured using your device camera, such as photos of completed work, job site conditions, and verification documents uploaded during onboarding.

Information collected automatically:

  • Location data collected with your consent when you use the app, to match you with nearby job opportunities, enable navigation to job sites, and display your availability in your service area.
  • Usage data such as app interactions, feature usage, taps, session duration, and in-app activity, collected to analyze product performance and improve the user experience.
  • Device and technical information such as device model, operating system version, IP address, and app diagnostics, used for security, fraud prevention, and crash reporting.
  • SMS verification data including your phone number and message delivery metadata, used for OTP/two-factor authentication.

Camera Access

FixerUp Pro requests access to your device camera to allow you to photograph and upload required verification documents - including your government-issued ID, insurance certificate, and bond proof - as part of the contractor onboarding process. The camera is also used to capture photos of job sites and completed work. Camera access is requested only when you actively initiate a document upload or photo capture within the app. Photos are transmitted securely to FixerUp's servers for verification purposes.

Location Access

FixerUp Pro requests access to your device location to match you with home service job opportunities in your area, display jobs near your current or preferred location, and enable navigation to job sites. Location is collected only when the app is in use and only with your explicit consent. You can withdraw location permission at any time through your device settings.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, verify, and secure your account
  • Verify your eligibility as a service professional through document review
  • Match you with relevant home service job opportunities in your area
  • Facilitate communication between you and homeowners
  • Process and facilitate payments for completed jobs
  • Send OTP/2FA verification codes via SMS
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and features
  • Comply with legal obligations

How We Share Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Services, including cloud infrastructure providers, SMS delivery providers, analytics providers, and payment processors.
  • Homeowners on the platform, limited to your name, professional profile, and job-related communications.
  • Telecommunications carriers as needed to deliver SMS verification messages.
  • Legal authorities when required to comply with applicable law, lawful requests, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and FixerUp.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Services and for legitimate business purposes such as security, compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement of agreements. Verification documents are retained in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support@fixerupus.com.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encrypted transmission of documents and credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

You may update your account information within the app. You may withdraw camera or location permissions at any time through your device settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact us at support@fixerupus.com.

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy and change the effective date. Continued use of the Services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

SMS Privacy

We use SMS only for OTP/2FA account verification. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. For support, contact support@fixerupus.com.
