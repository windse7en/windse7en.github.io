大厂最怕 AI 颠覆什么

What Big Tech Fears Most About AI Disruption

今日问题

Daily Question

大厂最怕被 AI 颠覆的核心是什么？

What is the core threat AI creates for big technology companies?

30 秒答案

30-Second Answer

AI 真正重定价的是优势结构：入口、资源优势、人才壁垒和组织速度。最危险的变化不是某个功能被替代，而是用户先向 AI 表达意图，原产品被推到后端执行层。

AI reprices the structure of advantage: entry points, resource advantages, talent barriers, and organizational speed. The dangerous shift is not one feature being replaced; it is users expressing intent to AI first while incumbent products move into the execution layer.

关键方向

Key Directions

  • 入口变化Entry Point Shift: 用户不一定再从原来的产品开始。AI 可能成为表达意图的第一站，然后决定后面调用哪个工具、服务或数据源。Users may stop starting inside the old product. AI can become the first place where intent is expressed, then decide which tool, service, or data source should be called next.
  • 资源优势被重新定价Resource Advantage Reset: 数据、品牌、渠道和资金仍然重要，但不再自动形成决定性护城河。小团队可以借助外部模型和工具完成过去需要大组织才能完成的工作。Data, brand, distribution, and capital still matter, but they no longer guarantee a decisive moat. Small teams can use external models and tools to do work that once required a large organization.
  • 人才壁垒被压缩Talent Barrier Compression: AI 放大优秀 builder、designer 和 founder 的产出。壁垒不是消失，而是从“人数和头衔”转向“判断力和速度”。AI amplifies the output of strong builders, designers, and founders. The barrier does not disappear; it moves from headcount and credentials toward judgment and speed.
  • 组织速度差Organizational Speed Gap: 大公司不一定输在技术，而可能输在组织速度：旧产品、收入保护、审批流程和品牌风险都会拖慢重组入口的速度。Large companies may not lose because of weak technology. They may lose because legacy products, business model protection, approval processes, and brand risk slow down their ability to reorganize the entry point.

Mental Model

Mental Model

谁拥有用户表达意图的入口，谁就拥有重新组织市场的机会。

Whoever owns the point where users express intent gets the chance to reorganize the market.

用户意图 → AI 入口 → 认知工作 → 执行层 → 结果User Intent → AI Entry Point → Cognitive Work → Execution Layer → Outcome

一句话记住

Memorable Quote

AI 最先拿走的不是服务本身，而是用户通往服务的入口。AI first takes the doorway to the service, not necessarily the service itself.
