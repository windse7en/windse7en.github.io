Daily questions, kept useful.

TaoThinks is Tao Zhang's personal publishing home for daily thinking around AI, products, and founder operating systems.

Publishing Principle

Use the blog as the source of truth, then adapt the same idea into Chinese and English social posts. The long version lives here; the public network gets the distilled version.

Confidentiality Filter

Posts should not expose FixerUp strategy, customer information, financial assumptions, sales pipeline, internal roadmap, hiring plans, or non-public operating data.

Archive Policy

Older technical posts are preserved for reference, but they are no longer promoted on the homepage, tag index, search index, or sitemap.
