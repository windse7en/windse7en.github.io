大厂最怕 AI 颠覆什么What Big Tech Fears Most About AI Disruption

AI 真正重定价的是优势结构：入口、资源优势、人才壁垒和组织速度。最危险的变化不是某个功能被替代，而是用户先向 AI 表达意图，原产品被推到后端执行层。

AI reprices the structure of advantage: entry points, resource advantages, talent barriers, and organizational speed. The dangerous shift is not one feature being replaced; it is users expressing intent to AI first while incumbent products move into the execution layer.
